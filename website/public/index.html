Wazy

Disney+ on your Mac, in a fast native app.

Download for Mac
Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon & Intel

How to install

DownloadClick the download button above. You get a .zip file in your Downloads folder.
UnzipDouble-click the .zip to unpack Wazy.
Move to ApplicationsDrag Wazy into your Applications folder, then open it.
Sign inSign in once with your own Disney+ account. Wazy remembers it.

Wazy is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other Mac app. To update, download the newest version here and replace the old one in Applications.
