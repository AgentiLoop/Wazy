Wazy Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · applies to Wazy for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Wazy is an independent app for watching your own Disney+ subscription. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Disney+ (The Walt Disney Company). Wazy has no accounts of its own, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking.

What Wazy collects

Nothing. The developer of Wazy does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information or usage data.

Your Disney+ sign-in

  • When you sign in, your email and password are sent directly from your device to Disney+ over an encrypted connection, the same way Disney+'s own website does it. They are never sent to the developer of Wazy.
  • Your email, password and the session Disney+ returns are kept on your device only, in the system Keychain and the app's settings, so you stay signed in. Signing out removes them.
  • What you browse and watch goes between your device and Disney+, under Disney+'s own privacy policy.

App Review demo account

For Apple's app review only, the demo username apple-review signs in through the developer's server at iwaz.xcf.ai instead of Disney+. Only that username is ever sent there; no other sign-in or user data goes to that server.

Settings kept on your device

Wazy remembers things like the volume, playback speed, subtitle choice, window size and what you watched last, so it can pick up where you left off. These stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Children

Wazy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: github.com/AgentiLoop/Wazy/issues.
